KickOra

Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 15, 2026

KickOra does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

KickOra does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal details.

The following data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to us:

  • Favorite teams you have saved
  • Your XP points and streak within the app
  • Quiz answers and scores
  • Cached football data (scores, standings, fixtures) for offline use
  • Your preferred app theme (dark or light mode)
  • Notification preferences

2. Football Data

Live scores, fixtures, standings, and team information are fetched from our server to display in the app. This data is publicly available sports information. No personal data is sent with these requests.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, KickOra may send you local notifications about match reminders. These are generated on your device and do not involve sending any personal data to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

KickOra does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not share any data with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

KickOra does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for all ages.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, your data is protected by your device's built-in security features. We have no access to your data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
